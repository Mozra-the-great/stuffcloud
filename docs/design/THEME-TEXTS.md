# Theme button texts

English source texts for the theme button (shown whenever a themed decoration set is
active). Translations follow the normal i18n catalogues. Keep the tone positive and
factual; no marketing copy.

## Winter

- From Dec 21 the days get longer again.
- Hedgehogs sleep through the cold months.

## New Year

- Happy New Year! A good moment to clear out old files and sites.
- People celebrate it with very different traditions around the world.

## Valentine's Day

- A day to tell people you care about them. Friends and family count too.
- Pixel hearts are free for everyone.

## Carnival

- A time for costumes, music and laughing together.
- It runs from Weiberfastnacht to Shrove Tuesday, shortly before Lent.

## Women's Day

- International Women's Day on March 8 celebrates women's achievements around the world.
- It has been observed since the early 1900s.

## Earth Day

- Earth Day on April 22 started in 1970 and is celebrated worldwide.
- Small things add up: your tools run in your own browser, not on a big server.

## Easter

- Eggs are an old symbol of new life and spring.
- Decorating eggs is a tradition in many countries.

## Europe Day

- May 9 recalls the Schuman Declaration of 1950, the start of European cooperation.
- The twelve stars on the flag stand for unity, not for a number of countries.

## Pride

- Pride celebrates everyone's right to be who they are and love who they love.
- stuffcloud is for everyone.

## Autumn

- Leaves turn red and orange when the green pigment fades and the other colours show.
- A good season for cosy projects.

## Unity Day

- October 3 marks German reunification in 1990.
- A day for celebrating what connects people.

## Harvest festival

- A day to say thank you for the harvest and for what we have.
- Apples, wheat and pumpkins are typical harvest symbols.

## Halloween

- A time for costumes and carving pumpkins.
- No tricks here: your files stay in your browser.

## Nicholas Day

- On December 6 children put out a boot and find small treats such as nuts and tangerines.
- Nicholas of Myra is remembered for helping people in need.

## Human Rights Day

- December 10 marks the day in 1948 when the Universal Declaration of Human Rights was adopted.
- Article 1: all human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights.

## Advent

- Advent counts down to Christmas: one more candle is lit each Sunday.
- Today: 3 of 4 candles.

## Christmas

- A time to be with the people you care about.
- The snow is only decoration: no tracking, no ads.
